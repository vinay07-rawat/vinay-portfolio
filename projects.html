🏔️ Devbhoomi - Uttarakhand Tourism & Data Analytics Portal

Data-Driven Tourism & Economic Impact Platform

Live Demo ↗

An advanced data analytics portal designed to highlight the historical, cultural, and economic importance of 22 key and hidden tourism destinations in Uttarakhand. Built to empower local homestays, tour guides, and regional handicraft businesses with digital identity and direct leads.

TECH STACK & TOOLS:

HTML5 & CSS3 (Glassmorphism) Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+) Chart.js (Data Analytics) WhatsApp API Integration GitHub Pages

KEY FEATURES:

  • Bilingual Smart Search: Instant search in English/Hindi and seasonal filter tags.
  • Dynamic UI & Glassmorphism: Dark/Light theme switch with translucent nature slide shows.
  • Data Matrix & Visualizations: Master database tracking footfall and revenue with Chart.js.
  • 4-Point Historical Insight: In-depth historical and economic contribution metrics for destinations.
  • Direct Booking Engine: Direct WhatsApp lead generation for booking itineraries.
